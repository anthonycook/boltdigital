Automating global logistics for a major tabletop games retailer.

We engineered a seamless bridge between Goblin Gaming's warehouse floor and major international couriers, completely transforming how they process their large daily orders.

Client
Goblin Gaming
Industry
Tabletop Games Retail
Tech Stack
Go (Golang)
Shopify GraphQL API

The Challenge

As a leading retailer in the tabletop gaming space, Goblin Gaming faced frustration with their existing infrastructure. It lacked the capability to handle complex international shipping rules and was prone to failure with non-standard addresses, which restricted their ability to sell globally and capped their potential profit margins.

The Solution

Bolt Digital stepped in to completely re-engineer their warehouse shipping integrations. We developed a highly optimised solution to resolve these bottlenecks.

Using a lightning-fast backend API written in Go, we created direct, real-time shipping integrations between their order management system and major couriers like DPD and Royal Mail.

By leveraging the Shopify GraphQL API and custom App Bridge development, the entire system synchronised perfectly with their existing storefront data.

"Our custom integrations allowed packing teams to instantly print and create shipments that would normally be booked in manually—turning a complex, multi-step chore into a single-click action."

The Results

The impact was immediate. The bespoke application eliminated manual data entry, drastically reducing human error and accelerating the dispatch queue. Hundreds of orders that previously took hours to process are now instantly converted into ready-to-ship packages with automated label printing.

Crucially, the new infrastructure successfully integrated complex international shipping rates and customs rules. This upgrade removed geographical barriers, allowing Goblin Gaming to confidently sell to a global audience and driving a significant increase in their overall profits.
